Privacy policy — imac-backup

Last updated: 18 October 2026.

  • imac-backup is used only by its owner, to back up the owner's own files to the owner's own Google Drive.
  • Files are encrypted on the owner's computer before upload. The tool's author and third parties never receive their content.
  • The tool requests only the Google Drive drive.file scope, so it can access only the files it created. It does not read other Drive content, mail or contacts.
  • No data is collected, sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
